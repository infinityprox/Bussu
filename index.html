Data sources & next steps

Start with published datasets and verify the licence, update date and permitted reuse before publishing.

  • CUMTA Transit Data Chennai — static GTFS datasets for Chennai public transport.
  • Tamil Nadu Open Government Data: SETC routes — published route, service and timing dataset.
  • TNSTC official website — official intercity reservation and service information.
This starter project does not claim official affiliation with MTC, TNSTC, SETC or CUMTA. Sample routes are placeholders and must not be used to make travel decisions.
